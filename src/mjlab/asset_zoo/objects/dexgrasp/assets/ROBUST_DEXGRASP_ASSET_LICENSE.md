# RobustDexGrasp training meshes

The collision meshes in the sibling object directories are the
`top_watertight_tiny.obj` meshes from the `new_training_set` released with
[RobustDexGrasp](https://github.com/zdchan/RobustDexGrasp). They are included
only for reproducing that teacher-training cohort in MuJoCo.
